About the author

Johannes Zauner is a human biologist and lighting designer. He studies how much light reaches the eye in everyday life and how to measure that exposure.

He is a postdoctoral researcher in the Translational Sensory and Circadian Neuroscience Unit (MPI / TUM / TUMCREATE) at the Technical University of Munich.

The study behind the demonstrator

Together with Kai Broszio and Karin Bieske, he investigated how indoor light measurements change when a radiance hood limits the sensor to the human field of view.
